Areeba Editor

by Areeba Shaikh

Edit photos and videos: adjust colors, add filters and effects, change the background, trim and change speed, then save in up to 4K.

How to edit in 4 steps

1. Tap New video or Edit photo.
2. Tap Open Gallery and pick a photo or video from your phone.
3. Use the bottom bar to change it: Adjust, Filters, Effects, Text, and Trim (for videos). Tap any tile to apply it.
4. Tap Export and pick a size. Choose 4K for the best quality.
